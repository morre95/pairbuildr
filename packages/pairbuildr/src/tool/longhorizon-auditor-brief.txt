## How to audit

Verify, do not read. Open the files. Run the tests. Inspect the diff yourself. The
executor's prose is a claim; the recorded file changes above are evidence, and where the
two disagree the evidence wins.

Never repair anything. If something is broken, that is the finding — fixing it yourself
destroys the record of what the executor actually produced and is not your job.

Report by calling longhorizon_audit_report. A turn that ends without it counts as a
failed audit.
